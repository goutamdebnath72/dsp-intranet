# Degrees and their levels

The table the system uses to understand a degree (`src/lib/qualifications/degrees.ts`). This document is generated from it: `npx tsx scripts/make-qualification-reference.ts > docs/QUALIFICATION_LEVELS.md`.

**The list is general knowledge of Indian degrees. It has NOT been checked against an official source.** The lines marked in the first section are the ones the author was unsure of.

## What a level means

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Certificate | school and trade certificates (Class X, Class XII, ITI) |
| Diploma (below graduate) | a diploma taken after Class X or XII (polytechnic engineering, D.Pharm, GNM ...) |
| Graduate | a bachelor's degree, or a professional degree of the same level (MBBS, BDS, B.Tech, BCA, B.Com, LLB ...) |
| Postgraduate | **everything higher than graduate**: a master's degree (M.Tech, MCA, MBA, M.Sc, LLM ...), MD, MS, DNB, MDS, and every POST-graduate diploma (DGO, DPM, PGDM, PGDCA ...) |
| Super-specialty | above MD / MS: DM, MCh, DrNB, FNB |
| Doctorate | PhD, DSc, D.Litt ... |

A question that says **post graduate** means every degree from Postgraduate upwards (so also super-specialty and doctorate). An **MD is postgraduate**, although it is called a doctor's degree.

## Notes

| Degree | Level used | Note |
|---|---|---|
| LLB | Graduate | Both the three-year LLB (after a bachelor's degree) and the five-year integrated forms are classed GRADUATE. |
| B.Ed | Graduate | Taken after a bachelor's degree, but it is a bachelor's degree by name: classed GRADUATE. |
| MD | Postgraduate | An MD is a POSTGRADUATE degree although it is called a doctor's degree. |
| MS | Postgraduate | 'MS' is also written for a Master of Science by research: both are postgraduate. |
| DNB | Postgraduate | Equal to MD / MS: classed POSTGRADUATE. |
| PG Diploma | Postgraduate | Any diploma that says POST graduate is classed POSTGRADUATE. |
| DM | Super-specialty (above MD / MS) | DM is a SUPER-specialty degree taken after an MD: above postgraduate. |
| MCh | Super-specialty (above MD / MS) | MCh is a SUPER-specialty degree taken after an MS: above postgraduate. |
| DrNB | Super-specialty (above MD / MS) | Equal to DM / MCh: classed SUPER-specialty. |
