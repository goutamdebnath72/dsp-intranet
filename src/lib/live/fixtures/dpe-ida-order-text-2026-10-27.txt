Office Memorandum
Subject: Revision of Industrial Dearness Allowance (IDA) Rates for Executives and
Non-Unionized Supervisors of CPSEs — effective from 01.10.2026.
The  undersigned  is  directed  to  convey  the  revised  rates  of  Industrial
Dearness Allowance (IDA) applicable to executives holding Board level and below
Board   level   posts   and   Non-Unionized   Supervisors   of   Central   Public   Sector
Enterprises (CPSEs) following the 1987, 1997, 2007 and 2017 pay scales, effective
from 01.10.2026.
2.         The  quantum  of  IDA  payable  from  01.10.2026  at  the  old  system  of
neutralization, for an increase of (215) points, may be Rs. 430/-. At Average AICPI
10069, DA payable may be Rs. 18,728 to the executives holding Board level post,
below Board level post and non-unionized supervisors following IDA pattern in the
CPSEs of 1987 pay scales.
3.            The  rates  of  Dearness  Allowance  (DA)  payable  w.e.f.  01.10.2026  to
executives  holding  Board-level  and  below  Board-level  posts,  as  well  as  non-
unionized  supervisors  of  CPSEs  following  the  1997,  2007,  and  2017  pay  scales,
shall be 489.5% for the 1997 pay scale, 249.2% for the 2007 pay scale, and 59.1%
for the 2017 pay scale.
4.                  All  administrative  Ministries/Departments  of  Government  of  India  are
requested   to   bring   the   foregoing   to   the   notice   of   the   CPSEs   under   their
administrative control for necessary action at their end.
5.         This issues with the approval of the Secretary, Department of Public
Enterprises.
